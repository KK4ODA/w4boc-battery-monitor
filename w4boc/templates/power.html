Mains power history

How it is detected: the Victron charger runs from mains and stops advertising over Bluetooth the instant AC drops, while the battery-powered BMS keeps answering (proving Bluetooth itself is fine). Charger silent for {{ cfg_fast }} min → one Bluetooth restart to rule out a stuck scanner → still silent {{ cfg_fast }} min later → MAINS LOST. A charger advertisement ends the outage. If the PC is on a USB-connected UPS, Windows reports AC loss instantly. A reboot after a gap with an "unexpected shutdown" in the Windows log is recorded as PC down (the PC itself lost power).
